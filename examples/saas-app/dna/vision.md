# Vision

## Mission

Standup Bot runs async standups for remote teams, so they get the
visibility of a daily meeting without the calendar tax.

## Goal

A team of 5–20 engineers, all in different timezones, can run a daily
standup that takes each person under 5 minutes and gives the manager
a 1-minute read on team state.

## Philosophy

- Async beats sync. Timezones make live meetings hostile.
- Defaults are kind. The bot prompts; people don't have to remember.
- The summary is the product. Individual updates are inputs; the
  digest is the output that matters.
- One team, one cadence. We don't try to support multiple
  standup schedules per team in v1.

## Non-goals

- Live meetings or video calls.
- Project management (no tickets, no sprints, no Kanban).
- 1:1 meeting facilitation.
- Cross-team standups.
- Custom workflows per team.
